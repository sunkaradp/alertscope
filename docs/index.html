System design

Designed around your existing observability stack

01 · Alert intake

Prometheus Alertmanager sends alert events to the FastAPI webhook.

02 · Evidence

Prometheus, Loki and optional Kubernetes collectors gather context.

03 · Investigation

Bedrock or Vertex can produce a structured first-pass analysis. Stub mode is explicitly demo logic.

04 · Operations

Incident lifecycle, evidence and timeline are persisted by the backend and shown here.
